KUBERNETES SERVICE NETWORK INCIDENT

Symptom
-------
Client Pod can resolve the Service DNS name, but HTTP traffic through
the Service fails.

Healthy Components
------------------
Client Pod
CoreDNS
Service DNS
Service selector
Backend Pods
Pod-to-Pod networking
nginx on TCP/80

Fault
-----
Service:
  port:       80
  targetPort: 8080

Container:
  listening port: 80

Observed Result
---------------
Service selected the correct Pods, but forwarded connections toward
TCP/8080 where nginx was not listening.

Diagnosis
---------
1. Verify client and server Pods are Ready.
2. Verify Service DNS resolution.
3. Verify Service selector matches intended Pods.
4. Inspect EndpointSlices.
5. Test backend Pod IP directly.
6. Compare Service targetPort with workload listening port.
7. Capture traffic with tcpdump.
8. Trace network paths as supporting evidence.
9. Correct targetPort.
10. Retest DNS name and ClusterIP.

Resolution
----------
Change Service targetPort:

  8080 -> 80

Final Result
------------
Service DNS request: SUCCESS
ClusterIP request:   SUCCESS
Direct Pod request:  SUCCESS
