restricted-test
---------------
enforce: restricted
audit: restricted
warn: restricted

baseline-test
-------------
enforce: baseline

privileged-test
---------------
enforce: privileged

warn: privileged

mixed-policy-test
-----------------
enforce: baseline
audit: restricted
warn: restricted

Behavior
--------
enforce:
  decides whether a workload is admitted or rejected

warn:
  returns policy warnings to the requesting client without blocking

audit:
  annotates admission decisions for audit logging when Kubernetes
  API audit logging is configured

Mixed-policy expectation:
  baseline-compliant workloads are admitted because enforce=baseline,
  while violations of restricted policy may still produce warnings
  and audit annotations.
