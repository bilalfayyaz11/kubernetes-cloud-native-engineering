External etcd HA validation

Topology:
- etcd1: 172.30.0.11
- etcd2: 172.30.0.12
- etcd3: 172.30.0.13

Transport:
- TLS enabled for client traffic
- TLS enabled for peer traffic
- client certificate authentication enabled
- peer certificate authentication enabled

Validated:
- 3-member cluster formation
- leader election
- endpoint health
- replicated write/read consistency
- one-member failure tolerance
- quorum writes with 2/3 members
- recovered member data synchronization

Failure simulation:
- etcd3 stopped
- etcd1 + etcd2 maintained quorum
- write operation succeeded
- etcd3 restarted
- failover data replicated to recovered member

Important scope:
This is a single-host process/container-level HA simulation and
does not provide independent machine or availability-zone fault isolation.
